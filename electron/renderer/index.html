    The folder holding baseq3\, and missionpack\ if Team Arena is installed. The maps and everything they draw are read straight out of the .pk3 archives.

      Conversion
      The window runs 1.79 to 1.94: below 100/56 the Killing Floor pawn no longer fits a Quake 3 passage, above 35/18 a stock staircase is taller than the engine's step height. 1.8634 is the middle of it.
      Every arch, pipe and hill in the game is a patch. 4 is the default; 2 halves their triangles and shows the facets.
      Quake 3's baked light is dark on purpose — its own renderer doubles it on load and the gamma ramp lifts it again. 4 puts it where this engine wants it.
      The two add up to what a surface is lit by. Splitting them keeps the world right without the player glowing.
      Blend the terrain's second layer
      Team Arena paints a second rock over the first and blends the two by the vertex alpha; without it a hillside is one flat texture. Costs a second pass over those surfaces only — measured on mpterra1 +3.6% triangles (+0.55 MB) and mpterra2 +6.5% (+0.57 MB); every other map is untouched.

        Lineage 2 client
        The folder holding maps\, staticmeshes\ and textures\. Packages are read straight out of it, encryption and all.
          One square becomes one map. Click to pick; picked squares convert in order.
          Conversion
          2 puts a Killing Floor pawn next to a Lineage 2 door the way a Lineage 2 character stands next to it.
          A square is 255×255 quads. 2 quarters the triangles and softens the ground.
          Open the doorways and cave mouths
          A doorway is not modelled: the wall is one brush and the hole through it is a subtractive brush the client's compiler cut away. Without this they all come across filled in. Costs nothing — no polygon is ever deleted, only cut.
          Blend the terrain layers
          The client's own per-layer blend, as a pass per layer. Without it the ground is one texture per quad and the seams show as squares. Costs about 3× the ground's triangles (19_21: 128k → 373k, +33 MB).
          Carry the grass across
          The square's own decoration layers, scattered to their own density. Tens of thousands of plants (19_21: 32k plants, 240k triangles, +41 MB) — the heaviest single thing on the map.
          The two add up to what a surface is lit by. Splitting them keeps the world right without the player glowing.
